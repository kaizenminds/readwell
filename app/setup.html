Set up Readwell

3 steps, about 4 minutes, once. Best done on a laptop. After that you can read on any phone, tablet or computer.

How this works. Readwell has no servers. You make your own copy of its small backend inside your Google account. It reads your Gmail as you, stores your highlights in a Sheet in your Drive, and nobody else, including us, can see any of it.

  1. Copy the backend 1 min

    • Open the template, signed in with the Google account you get newsletters in.
    • Click Overview (the i on the left), then Make a copy.
    Open the Readwell template
    Or build it by hand
    • Go to script.google.com and click New project.
    • Replace everything in Code.gs with this code.
    • Next to Services click +, pick Gmail API, click Add.
    • Save with Ctrl+S or Cmd+S.

  2. Deploy it 2 min

    • Click Deploy (top right), then New deployment.
    • Click the gear next to Select type and pick Web app.
    • Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
    • Click Authorize access and pick your account.
    • Google says "Google hasn't verified this app". That is expected: it's your own copy, not ours. Click Advanced, then Go to ... (unsafe), then Allow.
    Why "Anyone"? Is that safe?

    The Readwell app lives on a different website from Google, so it can't use your Google login to reach your backend. "Anyone" lets the app reach it. Your backend then refuses every request that doesn't carry your private 64-character key, which only ever goes to your own Gmail.

  3. Open the link, then check Gmail 1 min

    • Google now shows a Web app link. Click it.
    • Readwell sets itself up and emails you a message called Your Readwell link.
    • Open that email on the phone, tablet or laptop you read on and tap Open Readwell. Done.
    The same email connects every device: just tap the button on each one. Keep it private, it is your key.

Once you're in

  • Pick your newsletters. Open Senders. Tap Add for the ones you want: their last 5 days come in straight away, and new issues land every morning. Tap Ignore for the rest. Senders take a few minutes to appear the first time.
  • iPhone or iPad: if the email opens Readwell inside the Gmail app, tap the Safari icon first. Then Share, Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: Chrome menu, Install app. Laptop: the install icon in Chrome's address bar.

Good to know

Will it delete my mail?

Only if you ask. You can delete an issue yourself, and you can turn on auto-cleanup in Settings, which removes read issues after 5 days unless you saved, tagged, starred or highlighted them. It is off until you switch it on.

No email?

Look in Spam. Still nothing? Open your Web app link again after 10 minutes and a new one is sent. Find the link under Deploy, Manage deployments.

Lost a phone?

In your script, pick rotateApiKey and click Run. Every device is signed out and a new link arrives in your Gmail.

When does it check for new issues?

Once a day, at 7 AM in your timezone. Change the time in Settings. Check now runs it straight away.

"Could not reach your backend"

Check the deployment's access is Anyone. Then open your Web app link again (Deploy, Manage deployments) for a fresh email.

"Your backend refused this key"

The key was changed. Tap the button in your newest Your Readwell link email on each device.

I changed the script. Do I need to redeploy?

Yes: Deploy, Manage deployments, pencil icon, Version: New version, Deploy. The link stays the same.

How do I stop using Readwell?

In your script, Deploy, Manage deployments, Archive. Then delete the project and the "Newsletter Reader Data" Sheet. Your Gmail labels stay, so nothing is lost.
